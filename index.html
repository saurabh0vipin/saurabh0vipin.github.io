About Me

I am a passionate Backend Engineer with over 6 years of experience architecting and building high-performance, resilient, and cost-effective distributed systems.

My engineering journey is centered on solving hard scalability and latency problems. From building direct-to-farmer e-commerce portals handling country-wide operations to designing high-traffic fintech services for millions of daily active users, I design architectures that prioritize clean separation of concerns, robust monitoring, and optimization.
